User Manual

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use the HRMS app effectively.

Sign In & Workspace Access Which login screen opens first?
The app chooses the login route automatically based on what is already saved on the device. If there is no local account data, it opens the OTP sign-in flow. If an account is saved and an MPIN is already set, it opens the PIN login screen. If an account is saved but no MPIN is available yet, it opens the username and password screen.
How does OTP or password login work?
On the OTP flow, enter your work email address or mobile number, request the OTP, verify the code, and then confirm your workspace password. If multiple tenants are linked to the same identifier, the app asks you to choose the correct tenant before continuing. On the credentials flow, enter your username and password directly, and use Forgot Password? if you need a reset link.
When do I need to choose a company?
Internal users may be asked to choose a workspace after signing in. If only one company is available, the app can select it automatically. If more than one company is assigned to your account, open the company selection screen and choose the workspace you want before entering Home attendance mode.
How do PIN login and PIN recovery work?
Existing users can unlock the app with their saved 4-digit MPIN. In some setups, you may also need to choose the company and user before entering the PIN. If you tap Forgot PIN?, the app sends you back to the credentials login flow so you can sign in with your password and set up access again from the account side.
Attendance Camera & Daily Use How do I mark attendance?
Attendance is marked from the Home screen using the live camera and face recognition flow. Keep location access enabled, stay inside the face frame, and wait while the app counts down, captures the face, and shows the attendance result. The green attendance modal confirms whether the action was recorded as check-in or check-out.
What should I check before scanning?
Watch the location status banner at the top of Home. If location is blocked, fix location permissions or device settings first. You can also start or stop the recognizer from the header icon. If the app says the person is not enrolled or asks you to wait before another check-in or check-out, follow that message and try again after the configured delay.
How do I review attendance records?
Open Attendance Logs from the Home menu to browse saved records. Use the date controls to move between days, open the calendar picker for a specific date, and tap any row to view the full details including check-in time, check-out time, worked hours, saved face photo, and captured location.
What extra attendance options do admins get?
Admin users can switch between All Attendances and My Attendances from the logs screen. They can also use the sync action from the logs header to pull the latest attendance data for the selected day.
Profile, Enrollment & Settings What can I do from My Profile?
The Profile page shows your current company, employee name, role, active status, and whether your face is enrolled. From there you can start or update self-enrollment, jump back to the attendance camera, open your attendance history, refresh profile data, or remove the account from the current device.
How do I enroll or update my face?
Open Profile and use the self-enrollment action. If you are already enrolled, the same action lets you update your face image. Admin users can also open the Employees page, switch between enrolled and not-enrolled staff, search for a person, and open the enrollment flow for that employee.
What settings can I manage in the app?
The Settings page includes background reliability guidance, a link to app info for battery settings, language selection, check-in delay, check-out delay, sync delay, sound preference, MPIN change, and sign out. It also links to Server Configuration and Plan Details so you can review your tenant connection and subscription status.
Can I edit tenant connection details here?
No. The server configuration page is read-only in this app. It shows the saved plan, server URL, database name, company, username, password masking, and sync delay for the registered tenant connection currently stored on the device.
Registration, Offline Mode & Support How do I start if I am a new user?
If no local account is saved yet, the app opens the OTP entry flow first. From there you can continue with your tenant sign-in or use Register New Account if your organization is onboarding a new HRMS workspace. After registration or first login, finish company selection if prompted and then proceed to Home.
What happens when the device is offline?
The app shows a dedicated offline page when network access is unavailable and keeps checking for reconnection automatically. Attendance and employee data are stored locally for the selected company, and syncing resumes when the device comes back online and the normal app flow continues.
What should I do if I see plan or license warnings?
Login screens can show tenant or license notices when the workspace needs attention. Use the renewal action if your flow provides it, or open Settings > Plan Details to review the current plan, tenant status, expiry information, and the latest saved workspace details.
Where can I get help from inside the app?
Use the Help button on the login screens to open this manual at any time. If you need direct assistance, open the Contact page and send your details to the support team, or contact your organization’s HR or IT team for tenant-specific access issues.
